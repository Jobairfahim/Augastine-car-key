Ignition Repair & Replacement

If your car key will not turn, gets stuck in the ignition, or the vehicle refuses to start, the problem may be the ignition system. We provide professional ignition repair and replacement services at your location.

  • ⚠️
    Common Ignition Problems
    Key not turning, key stuck in ignition, ignition difficult to turn, broken key inside ignition, worn ignition cylinder, vehicle not starting, ignition switch problems.
  • 🔍
    Ignition Diagnosis
    We inspect ignition cylinder condition, key wear or damage, steering lock interference, internal lock components, and electrical ignition response before recommending a solution.
  • 🔧
    Ignition Repair Service
    When possible, repair is the most cost-effective option. Includes cleaning internal components, adjusting worn parts, removing broken key pieces, restoring smooth key rotation.
  • 🔄
    Ignition Replacement
    If severely worn or damaged, we remove the faulty ignition, install a new unit, match keys when possible, program for newer vehicles if required, and test full operation.
  • 🚐
    Mobile Ignition Service
    Our fully mobile service allows us to complete ignition repairs and replacements at homes, workplaces, parking lots, and roadside locations — saving time and avoiding towing.

⚙️ Signs You Need Ignition Service

  • Key will not turn in ignition
  • Key gets stuck and won't come out
  • Ignition is very stiff or hard to turn
  • Broken key piece stuck inside
  • Worn or damaged ignition cylinder
  • Vehicle refuses to start
  • Ignition switch failure

Transponder Key Programming & Key Duplication

Many vehicle owners wait until they lose their only key before getting a spare. Creating a duplicate key while you still have a working original is usually faster, easier, and more convenient.

  • 🧠
    What Is a Transponder Key?
    A transponder key contains a small electronic chip inside the key head that communicates with your vehicle's immobilizer system. Even if the metal key turns, the vehicle may not start unless the chip is programmed correctly.
  • ✂️
    Car Key Copy & Duplication
    Duplicate car keys, spare ignition keys, backup door keys, laser cut key copies, and high-security key duplication — copies that work smoothly and reliably.
  • 🚗
    Programming Keys Bought Online
    Many customers purchase keys online and need them cut or programmed after delivery. In many cases we can help if the key is compatible with your vehicle's make, model, and key type.
